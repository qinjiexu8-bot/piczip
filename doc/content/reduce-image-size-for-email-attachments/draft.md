# How to Reduce Image Size for Email Attachments

The approved article is implemented at:

`src/app/(en)/guides/reduce-image-size-for-email-attachments/page.tsx`

## Editorial decisions

- The article does not claim one universal email attachment limit.
- It starts with the recipient's intended use, not an arbitrary compression percentage.
- The experiment uses mixed image content and reports every original and output size.
- ZIP is described as packaging, supported by the measured 58-byte overhead.
- It distinguishes screen viewing from print or editing delivery.
- It explains when individual attachments are easier than an archive.
- Provider limits are discussed without inventing current product-specific numbers.
