# Content Brief: Why Is My Compressed Image Still Too Large?

Status: Approved

- Primary keyword: `why is my compressed image still too large`
- Intent: Diagnose a compression result that remains above an upload limit
- Audience: Users who already tried compression but still cannot upload the result
- Problem: Identify whether format, target behavior, or pixel dimensions are preventing a useful reduction
- Matching tools: `/compress-png` and `/`
- Secondary queries: `compressed photo still too big`, `image compressor not reducing file size`
- Distinct purpose: Troubleshooting failed or disappointing results rather than teaching a generic compression workflow
- Main internal link: `/compress-png`
- Original value: One failed PNG result and one successful width-limited WebP result show why retrying the same settings is ineffective
- Cannibalization rule: Keep diagnosis central; do not target broad `compress png` or fixed-size landing-page intent
