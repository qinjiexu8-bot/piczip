# How to Reduce PNG Size for an Email Signature

Publication route: `/guides/reduce-png-size-for-email-signature`

The article tells readers to match the delivery copy to its rendered width before forcing a byte target. It distinguishes an embedded signature image from a one-time email attachment and requires testing in real mail clients.

An 800×600, 1,617,783-byte RGBA fixture became a 320×240 PNG of 130,815 bytes. A WebP copy at the same dimensions measured 17,220 bytes. The article recommends PNG when compatibility is uncertain and treats 320 pixels as a reproducible example, not a universal standard.

The public route contains the complete copy, two PicZip screenshots, numbered steps, limitations, FAQ, testing note, About link, correction contact, and CTA to `/compress-png`.
